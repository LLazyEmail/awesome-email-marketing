# Free email marketing tool by Google

The original notes point at Feedburner as a $0 way to email subscribers when new blog posts go out.

Claimed capabilities from those notes:

1. Delivers new blog posts to subscribers by email.
2. People can subscribe from the website.
3. You can export the list.
4. It is free.

Tool: [feedburner.google.com](https://feedburner.google.com)

The notes also mention pairing it with Sumo Welcome Mat.

- [Welcome Mat by Sumo](https://sumo.com/app/welcome-mat)
- [Feedburner help](https://support.google.com/feedburner/answer/78982)

Feedburner is a legacy Google product. Confirm it still fits the use case before relying on it for a live list.
