# 60% open rate for sales emails

This company sold air conditioners and also sent daily weather forecasts by email and text.

They sent sales emails asking people to buy an air conditioner only when the temperature crossed **75°F (24°C)**.

Reported results: **60%** of those emails and close to **90%** of the text messages were opened.

The idea is contextual timing: sell when the message matches what the subscriber is already experiencing.
