# 3.5 million opt-ins analysis

Sumo analyzed over 3.5 million opt-ins from scroll pop-ups and reported a depth that produced the best conversions.

Scroll pop-ups appear after a visitor reaches a certain point on an article, landing page, or product page.

The reported best place to show the scroll pop-up is **47.7%** down the page.

Source: [Sumo — attention-grabbing popups](https://sumo.com/stories/attention-grabbing-popups)
