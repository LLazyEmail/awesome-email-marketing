# +271% more opt-ins (study)

How RocketMemory increased opt-ins by +271% without reducing the number of form fields.

RocketMemory had low conversions for opt-ins: 5.15%. They could not reduce the number of fields in their form.

They asked for:

- First name
- Email
- Age
- Gender
- Are you a student: yes/no
- Are you taking classes or studying for something? yes/no

After they split the flow into 4 micro-commitment steps, signup rate increased by 271% (more than triple).

## The four steps

1. **"Would you like to receive memory techniques specifically geared to what you're studying?"** — Yes/No
2. **"Are you a man or a woman?"** — 2 options
3. **"Which of the following describes you?"** — 4 options
4. **"Where Should I Send Your Personalized Memory Tips?"** — name and email

Same information, asked across four smaller commitments.

Source: [Crazy Egg — science of micro-commitments](https://www.crazyegg.com/blog/science-of-micro-commitments)
